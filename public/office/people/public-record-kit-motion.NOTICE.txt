Office character: public_record assembled from ready-made kit parts by tools/newsroom/assets/kit-cast/recipes/public-record-kit.json, on the canonical 66-joint rig.
Surface, textures and skin weights: Quaternius, Universal Base Characters (Standard, free) and Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy (Standard, free), CC0 1.0 (provenance and archive hashes in tools/newsroom/assets/kit-cast/provenance/). Textures recoloured and geometry cut, lengthened and layered by tools/newsroom/assets/kit-cast/assemble-kit.py; small props and modern shirt details are generated there.
The canonical rig and library motions are Mesh2Motion assets, CC0 1.0.
Recorded walking, laptop typing and coffee sipping originate in the CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database; BVH conversion by Bruce Hahne.
The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.
